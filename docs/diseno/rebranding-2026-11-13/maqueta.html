About

I'm a data scientist with an unusual background: I hold a degree in Psychology with a specialization in Neurosciences (Universidad Favaloro), and I'm completing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at UdeSA. That combination shapes how I design products and research.

My core interest is AI for mental health: using machine learning to make sense of brain signals, clinical data and behavioral patterns. I've co-authored indexed publications in neuroscience and behavioral science, and I teach AI for psychology and clinical settings.

Today I work as Neuroscience and AI Specialist at Humai, leading educational strategy and advanced AI training. I'm also a freelance data analyst at Universidad Favaloro's Vocational Psychology department.

Technically I live between data engineering (pipelines, dbt, Airflow), applied ML (PyTorch, LLMs, RAG) and product (Next.js, FastAPI). I care about code that ships, and documentation that helps the next person understand why.
