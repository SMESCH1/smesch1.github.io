About

I'm a 27-year-old Data Scientist and Licensed Psychologist specializing in cognitive neurosciences, currently completing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at UdeSA. This blend of behavioral insight and deep learning architectures structures how I build algorithms, data systems, and educational platforms.

My research focus sits firmly at the crossroads of AI and mental health—leveraging machine learning pipelines to analyze smartphone telemetry records (Project Atenti), trace digital addiction clusters, and parse complex neuroimaging biomarkers (fMRI). I routinely collaborate with scientific organizations, including CNC/LITERA labs, as an adjunct researcher for Universidad Favaloro.

Professionally, I operate as the Academic Coordinator for Humai, orchestrating EdTech strategy, executing student retention analytics, and leading large-scale system transitions—including our recent 2026 migration from Open edX to a proprietary ecosystem. I actively execute an optimized "vibe coding" loop, harnessing advanced AI agents like Cursor, Claude Code, and local Ollama layers to refactor and build out high-performance pipelines.

Technically, I bridge applied multi-LLM workflows, robust analytics tracking, and product deployments. Beyond code, I engage in electronic music production, hybrid DJ setups using Digital Vinyl Systems (DVS), and collecting indoor plants like Ficus elastica and Philodendrons.
